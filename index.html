Welcome to WildGuard

Wildlife plays an important role in maintaining the balance of nature. Every animal, bird and plant contributes to our ecosystem. However, habitat destruction, pollution, climate change and illegal hunting threaten many wildlife species. WildGuard is a platform dedicated to spreading awareness about wildlife conservation and encouraging people to protect nature.
