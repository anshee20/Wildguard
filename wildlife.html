Explore Wildlife

Discover the diversity of life on Earth.

Mammals

Mammals are warm-blooded animals that generally have hair or fur and feed their young with milk.

Examples: Tigers, elephants and deer.

Birds

Birds are animals with feathers, beaks and wings. Most birds can fly.

Examples: Eagles, peacocks and parrots.

Aquatic Animals

Aquatic animals live in water and are important for maintaining aquatic ecosystems.

Examples: Dolphins, whales and fish.

Reptiles

Reptiles are cold-blooded vertebrates with scales. Most lay eggs.

Examples: Snakes, crocodiles and turtles.

Importance of Wildlife

  • Maintains ecological balance.
  • Supports food chains and food webs.
  • Helps in pollination and seed dispersal.
  • Maintains biodiversity.
  • Supports healthy ecosystems.

Different Wildlife Habitats

Wildlife lives in different habitats, including forests, grasslands, deserts, oceans, wetlands and mountains. Each habitat provides food, shelter and suitable living conditions.
